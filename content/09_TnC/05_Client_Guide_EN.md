**Week 1 (Days 1-7): Contract and first payment**

- *oBacker does:* issues the internal financial statements for the first month; issues the first-month payslips; schedules the month-end review meeting.
- *The Client does:* reviews the reports within three business days; gives feedback on the collaboration process.

## 5. Basic tax and accounting principles the Client should know

Every business operating lawfully in Vietnam must follow the basic compliance principles below, whether or not it uses oBacker's services.

**5.1. Having a company means filing reports, even before any activity begins.** Filing obligations start the moment an ERC is issued:

- Value Added Tax (VAT) return (monthly or quarterly): a nil (zero) return must still be filed on time.
- Personal Income Tax (PIT) return: not required if no income is paid to any individual; however, oBacker recommends filing to avoid late-filing penalties should a supplementary return be required later.
- Corporate Income Tax (CIT) finalisation and annual financial statements: mandatory even with no transactions.

*Consequences of missing the deadline:* administrative penalty under Decree No. 125/2020/ND-CP, with possible enforcement through deactivation of the tax code or freezing of bank accounts.

**5.2. Every expense needs a valid contract and supporting documents.** To be deductible for CIT, an expense must simultaneously: relate to production and business activity; have lawful invoices and supporting documents; and have non-cash payment evidence for any expense of VND 5 million or more.

**5.3. Payments to individuals.** Every payment to an individual; including someone who is not a formal employee; requires an appropriate contract (labour contract, service contract, collaborator contract or asset-lease contract), payment evidence, and PIT withholding and declaration according to the nature of the payment:

- Salary and bonuses under a labour contract: withheld under the progressive tax schedule.
- Occasional/irregular income under a service or collaborator contract: 10% withholding if the payment is VND 5 million or more per instance.
- Leasing an individual's assets (property, vehicles, etc.): declared in all cases; 5% VAT and 5% PIT withheld on the portion of rental revenue exceeding the non-taxable revenue threshold as prescribed.

If you pay an individual without a contract or without withholding PIT, the expense is disallowed as a deductible cost, and the business faces a PIT clawback assessment plus penalties and a late-payment surcharge.

**5.4. Long-term employment requires social insurance registration.** Under the 2019 Labour Code and the Law on Social Insurance No. 41/2024/QH15 (effective 1 July 2025):

- Labour contracts of one month or more: mandatory participation in social insurance (SI), health insurance (HI) and unemployment insurance (UI).
- Collaborators or freelancers who work regularly in a manner resembling employment: the social insurance authority may issue a back-assessment if an inspection finds it is effectively an employment relationship.
- Foreign employees with a work permit of one year or more: mandatory social insurance participation, except where exempt under a bilateral agreement.

*Consequences of not registering or paying insurance for eligible persons:* back-assessment of the entire unpaid amount plus late-payment interest of 0.03% per day under the 2024 Law on Social Insurance; administrative fines on the enterprise ranging from VND 10,000,000 to VND 150,000,000 based on the number of undeclared employees (or 12% to 20% for payment violations) under Decree No. 283/2026/ND-CP; compensation to employees for statutory benefits in the event of illness, accident or maternity; and potential criminal liability for insurance evasion under Article 216 of the Penal Code.

*Recommendation:* if the business uses the same collaborator continuously for at least one month with a steady workload, convert them to a formal labour contract.

**5.5. When a business must issue an invoice.** Under Decree No. 254/2026/ND-CP and Circular No. 91/2026/TT-BTC, a business must issue an e-invoice when: selling goods or providing services (issued at the moment of delivery or completion, regardless of whether payment has been received); receiving an advance or deposit for services; giving, gifting, exchanging or paying salary in kind; lending, borrowing or repaying; and internal consumption.

| Situation | Misconception | Correct |
|---|---|---|
| Retail customer does not want an invoice | "No need to issue" | Must still issue |
| Transaction under 200,000đ | "No need to issue each one" | Must still issue each transaction separately |
| The Client has not paid | "Issue only once paid" | Must issue upon delivery or service completion |
| Long-term contract | "Issue once at contract end" | Issue per acceptance milestone or upon receiving payment |

*Consequences of not issuing or issuing at the wrong time:* administrative penalties for invoicing under Decree No. 125/2020/ND-CP (the amount depends on the act and circumstances and is determined by the specific provision); a VAT and CIT clawback on the unreported revenue; and possible aggravation if it is deemed tax evasion.

**5.6. Paying for foreign software or services requires Foreign Contractor Tax declaration.** When a Vietnamese business pays a foreign supplier (with no legal presence in Vietnam) for software, services, licences, hosting or advertising, it is obliged to withhold and pay Foreign Contractor Tax (FCT) on the supplier's behalf. Legal basis: Circular No. 69/2025/TT-BTC Article 9 (value-added tax) and Circular No. 20/2026/TT-BTC Article 7 (corporate income tax). This applies to every payment method: credit or debit card, international transfer, payment intermediaries, or direct deduction from a balance on a foreign platform.

Typical transactions requiring declaration: subscription software (for example, design tools, source-code hosting, cloud office suites); online advertising; foreign hosting, domain and content-delivery-network services; purchase of content, image or font licences; hiring foreign individuals or firms; and brokerage commissions paid to foreign partners.

> The FCT rate, and whether a specific supplier has already declared any portion, depends on the type of transaction, the contract and the timing; oBacker determines this when handling each case. Common reference rates: cloud-based subscription software is subject to 10% VAT and 5% CIT; licensed software not on a cloud platform is subject to 10% CIT (no VAT); online advertising is subject to 10% VAT and 5% CIT.

*What the Client must do when it arises:* notify oBacker immediately through the assigned Account Manager or via email at contact@obacker.com right after the transaction, attaching the invoice or receipt, card statement or payment evidence, and a description of the service. The invoice must show the correct tax code and address of the Vietnamese company. oBacker will obtain a contractor tax code (if not already held), and file and pay by the deadline.

| Declaration method | Applies when | Filing deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Per instance (default) | Applies to each payment made to the foreign contractor where the enterprise has not registered for monthly filing | No later than the 10th day from the day following the date payment arises, per Circular No. 89/2026/TT-BTC Article 30 clause 2 point a and Decree No. 252/2026/ND-CP Article 10 clause 1 |
| Monthly (registered option) | Applies where the enterprise makes multiple payments in a month and has registered for monthly filing with the tax authority | No later than the 20th day of the following month, per Circular No. 89/2026/TT-BTC Article 30 clause 2 point a and Decree No. 252/2026/ND-CP Article 10 clause 2 (no return required for months with no withholding) |

*Consequences of not declaring or declaring late:* a back-assessment of all unpaid tax; a penalty of 20% of the tax; a late-payment surcharge of 0.03% per day; and possible disallowance of the foreign-service expense as a deductible cost.

## 6. Services included in the packages

**Accounting (all three packages).** Book-keeping based on the documents the Client provides; monthly closing; monthly internal financial statements; annual financial statements (balance sheet, income statement, cash-flow statement, notes); on-time filing of the financial statements. *Documents needed in the first month:* ERC; IRC (if a foreign-invested enterprise); the charter; the most recent annual financial statements; the trial balance; the general journal; the list of fixed assets with depreciation; contracts in effect; the last 12 months of statements for all accounts (with file passwords); and login information or access to the e-invoice system.

**Tax (all three packages).** VAT declaration and payment (monthly or quarterly); CIT provisional payment and finalisation; PIT withholding and filing on occasional income paid to individuals outside the payroll; FCT declaration when it arises (quota by package: Partner Core 01 contract/month, Partner Growth and Partner Prime 03 contracts/month). *Pre-deadline coordination (D is the due date):* at D-10, oBacker sends the draft return; at D-7, the Client confirms; at D-5, oBacker files through the e-tax system; at D-3, the Client pays the tax (if any). If the D-7 milestone passes without the Client's confirmation, oBacker's delivery timeline is temporarily suspended pursuant to Article 5.4(b) of the Master T&C. oBacker has the right to file provisionally based on available data or submit a nil return to secure statutory deadlines; the Client bears 100% of all subsequent amendment costs and late-payment penalties arising from delayed confirmation. *The Client undertakes to:* send all documents before the 5th of each month; pay tax on time (oBacker only files and does not pay on the Client's behalf unless there is a linked-account arrangement); and not adjust returns on the tax system without informing oBacker.

**HR and payroll (all three packages).** Drafting labour contracts, addenda and non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) from standard templates; registering individual tax codes and dependants; running monthly payroll and issuing payslips; withholding, filing and finalising PIT on salaries; registering and adjusting social insurance, health insurance and unemployment insurance; issuing PIT withholding certificates; and quick advice on payroll and insurance rules.

*New-hire process:* the Client sends the information (full name and citizen ID number, position, start date, probation and official salary, probation period, contract type, allowances and bonuses); the Client sends the information form for the employee to complete; oBacker drafts the labour contract and NDA; the Client signs with the employee; oBacker registers social insurance, health insurance and unemployment insurance within 30 days of the labour-contract signing date.

*Payroll cycle (D is the fixed timesheet cut-off day chosen by the Client):* on day D, the Client sends the variables (attendance, leave, overtime, bonuses); at D+3, oBacker prepares the payslips and sends them for review; at D+4, the Client reviews and confirms; at D+5, the Client pays salaries.

*HR changes:* the Client notifies oBacker at least five business days in advance of: new hires, resignations, salary increases or decreases, position or contract changes, maternity, and long-term sick leave.

> **On payroll calculation:** oBacker calculates payroll using a standard Payroll Framework agreed by both parties in advance. If the business has complex, highly customised payroll or timekeeping (bespoke multi-variable formulas, complex target-based or commission bonus schemes, non-standard shifts), the parties agree on one of three options: the Client provides figures with the complex parts pre-processed; or the rules are simplified so they fit within the Framework; or it is handled as an out-of-package add-on service.

> oBacker does not provide employment-termination, disciplinary or labour-dispute services in any of the three packages (see Section 8).

**E-invoice and digital signature (all three packages).** Registration and activation of the e-invoice system under Decree No. 254/2026/ND-CP and Circular No. 91/2026/TT-BTC; set-up of invoice symbols and number ranges; advice on e-invoice rules. oBacker does not issue output invoices on the Client's behalf; the Client issues them on the software that has been set up. If the Client wants oBacker to issue them, this is an out-of-package add-on service, charged separately.

*Digital Signature USB Token policy:* When the Client uses oBacker's back-office operations services, the Client gives oBacker 01 secondary USB Token with a limited written authorisation for operations within the contracted service scope (tax filing, e-invoicing, social insurance reporting). The Client retains the primary USB Token for executive banking and commercial transactions. oBacker holds only the secondary USB Token that the Client gives, in a secure environment, and logs each use.

**Legal support (tiered by package).**

- *Partner Core:* access to the document template platform to use all standardised document and contract templates; electronic document storage. Does not include contract review or legal advisory (available under the master service catalog).
- *Partner Growth:* as Partner Core, plus 2 commercial contracts reviewed per month (up to 10 pages), 2 ERC changes/year, and drafting of 6 internal documents/year.
- *Partner Prime:* as Partner Growth, plus 5 commercial contracts reviewed per month (up to 10 pages), 4 ERC changes/year, drafting of 12 internal documents/year, and a Dedicated Account Manager.

*Scope of one contract review:* one contract of up to 10 pages (roughly 3,000 to 4,000 words); a check of basic legal risk and flagging of unfavourable clauses; one review round and one feedback round; turnaround of three business days. A contract running from 11 to 20 pages counts as two reviews; a contract over 20 pages is charged VND 100,000 per page from page 21, or moves to an add-on service. *The Client should provide:* the contract (a Word or PDF file with selectable text), the transaction context, the Client's position, and any clauses of particular concern.

## 13. Frequently asked questions

**A newly established company that is not yet operating; does it still need to file tax returns?**
Yes. Filing obligations arise the moment the ERC is issued. A nil return must still be filed on time (see Section 5.1).

**I pay a collaborator for a three-month project with no labour contract; what do I need to do?**
You need a service contract, payment evidence and 10% PIT withholding (if the payment is VND 5 million or more per instance). If this person works continuously and regularly, convert to a labour contract (see Sections 5.3 and 5.4).

**A retail customer does not want an invoice; so I can skip issuing one, right?**
Not correct. You must still issue the invoice, recording the buyer's name and address (see Section 5.5).

**I paid for online advertising and foreign software by company card; do I need to notify oBacker?**
You must notify us immediately after the transaction. oBacker will determine whether the transaction is subject to FCT declaration. If you do not notify us and it is not declared by the statutory deadline, the business will face penalties and a back-assessment (see Section 5.6).

**Does the Partner Core package include drafting labour contracts for new employees?**
Yes. Drafting standard labour contracts and NDAs for internal employees applies to all three packages. It does not include drafting commercial contracts with third parties.

**The Partner Growth package has 2 contract reviews per month; if I do not use them this month, do I get 4 next month?**
No. The quota does not carry over to the next month. If you need more, you can order additional reviews under the master service catalog prices.

**Can I contact oBacker staff directly through personal messaging?**
Not recommended. All official exchanges go through the Account Manager by email so they are traceable and logged in the system. Personal messaging is for quick exchanges only, is not related to deliverables, and is not officially recorded.

**What do I do if I find an error in a report oBacker sent?**
Reply immediately on the email that contains that report, stating the error clearly and attaching supporting documents. oBacker responds and resolves it within 24 business hours. If the error results in a penalty, the responsibility-allocation mechanism in Section 10 applies.

**How do I upgrade from Partner Core to Partner Growth or Partner Prime?**
Email the assigned Account Manager or contact@obacker.com. oBacker sends a contract addendum within three business days; the upgrade takes effect from the next reporting cycle. The first month's fee after the upgrade is charged at the new package rate, and no refund is given for the previously paid fee of the old package.

**What happens if the business exceeds the transaction or employee threshold of its current package?**
oBacker applies its Fair Use Policy (FUP). Upon reaching or exceeding the monthly quota, oBacker reconciles and invoices monthly post-billing add-on transaction blocks (ADD-TXN-BLOCK-500-2026, ADD-TXN-BLOCK-1000-2026, ADD-TXN-BLOCK-1500-2026). Businesses exceeding the 1,500 transactions/month hard ceiling must upgrade to Partner Prime or bespoke custom contracts.

**I need to handle a dispute with a former employee; does oBacker help?**
No. Labour disputes fall under the out-of-scope group (Section 8). oBacker can refer you to a lawyer specialising in labour matters if you request it.

**How is my data protected?**
oBacker complies with the Law on Personal Data Protection No. 91/2025/QH15 and the service contract. Data is stored on a system with access controls; only assigned staff can view it. See the Personal Data Protection Policy published at obacker.com for details.

**What happens when the services are terminated?**
oBacker hands over all books, records and system login details to the Client or the new accounting provider within 15 business days of the termination date.
