# PERSONAL DATA PROTECTION POLICY
### Including a dedicated section on Sensitive Personal Data

**Applies to:** oBacker Joint Stock Company
**Version:** R.1.0.0 (VI-EN) · **Issue date:** 21 September 2026 · **Effective Date:** 21 September 2026

---

When you choose to work with oBacker; whether as a corporate client, a client's employee, a shareholder, a partner, or a website user; you place a great deal of private and sensitive information in our hands. We understand the value of that trust and do not take it lightly.

oBacker publishes this Policy so you can check, at any time: what we collect, why, what we do with the data, who can access it, and how you can intervene. In short: the data is yours; oBacker is simply the party you have entrusted to process it.

The Policy has two parts. **Part I; General Provisions** applies to all personal data. **Part II; Sensitive Personal Data** sets out enhanced measures. Where there is any difference concerning sensitive data, **Part II prevails**.

## Article 2. Key terms

- **Personal data:** information in the form of symbols, writing, digits, images, sound, or similar forms that is attached to or helps identify a specific individual. It includes both basic and sensitive data.
- **Basic personal data:** full name, date of birth, gender, place of residence, telephone number, email, account information, online activity data, and other information as provided by law.
- **Sensitive personal data:** data linked to privacy which, if violated, directly affects an individual's lawful rights and interests (detailed in Part II).
- **Data Subject:** the individual reflected by the personal data.
- **Data Controller:** an organisation or individual that decides the purpose and means of processing.
- **Data Processor:** an organisation or individual that processes data on behalf of the Data Controller under a contract or agreement.
- **Data Controller-and-Processor:** an entity that both decides the purpose and means and directly carries out the processing.
- **Processing of personal data:** collecting, recording, analysing, storing, correcting, disclosing, combining, accessing, retrieving, recalling, encrypting, copying, sharing, transmitting, transferring, deleting, or destroying data, and the like.
- **Consent:** a clear, voluntary, and affirmative expression by the Data Subject permitting the processing of their data.
- **DPIA:** Data Protection Impact Assessment dossier. **DTIA:** Cross-border Data Transfer Impact Assessment dossier.

# PART II; SENSITIVE PERSONAL DATA

Much of the data oBacker processes day to day is sensitive personal data; bank account details, salary levels, capital ownership ratios, social insurance contributions, health status related to maternity/sick leave, and biometric data used when signing documents. This is information that, if violated, can directly affect your rights, assets, and private life. For that reason, oBacker applies a higher level of protection. Part II applies alongside Part I; where there is any difference concerning sensitive data, **Part II prevails**.

## Article 21. What sensitive personal data is

Data linked to privacy which, if violated, directly affects an individual's lawful rights and interests. Under Vietnamese law, it includes: political and religious views; health status and private life in medical records; racial and ethnic origin; genetic characteristics and distinctive physical/biological attributes; biometric data (fingerprints, iris, facial data, electronic signatures with biometric elements, etc.); sexual life/orientation; data on crimes and unlawful conduct; financial information classified as sensitive under applicable law; location data; and other data classified as sensitive by law.

## Article 23. Processing grounds and separate consent

Where processing is based on consent,** oBacker: clearly states before collection that the data to be processed is sensitive; obtains **SEPARATE, DISTINCT** consent for each type of data and each purpose; not bundled with basic data; ensures the consent is voluntary, clear, and specific; does **NOT** use default/pre-ticked consent boxes; processes only within the scope notified; and retains evidence of consent (timestamp, channel, content). **23.2. In these cases, oBacker: records the specific legal ground; assesses the risk beforehand (via a DPIA); applies the enhanced measures under Article 25; and retains evidence of internal approval for each decision.

## Article 25. Enhanced protection measures

**25.1. Technical measures:** encryption at rest and in transit (TLS); **mandatory MFA** for every account accessing systems that hold sensitive data; access limited on a need-to-know basis, role-based permissions, and access logging; **immutable/tamper-resistant logs** for access and operations; **approval and logging before exporting** sensitive data; and **masking/tokenisation** when using sensitive data for testing, analysis, or training.

**25.2. Organisational measures:** appointing a DPO/responsible unit; preparing a **dedicated DPIA** for each sensitive-data processing activity before deployment; in-depth training for staff who handle sensitive data; a **Sensitive Data Access Register** (who accessed what, when, and for what purpose); **quarterly access-rights reviews**; and a **Security Exception Register** recording exceptions that do not yet meet the required measures, together with a remediation plan.

**25.3. Mapping and governance.** Each type of sensitive data is mapped to: Data Mapping/ROPA; a DPIA; a Retention Schedule; and a list of authorised persons/units with the reason and level of access.
