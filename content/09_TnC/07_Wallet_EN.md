## Part 1; What the oBacker Wallet is and how it works

**The oBacker Wallet is a prepaid balance** tied to the Client's account on the oBacker platform. The Client tops up the Wallet, and the system **automatically deducts** service fees from this balance at each billing cycle.

**How it works:**

1. **Log in and enter the amount** you wish to top up on the platform.
2. **Pay** by bank transfer or payment gateway.
3. **The balance is credited to the Wallet** once oBacker confirms receipt of the funds.
4. **The system automatically deducts** the corresponding service fee when the cycle falls due.

## Part 2; Detailed terms

**1.** The top-up function is available only to Clients (Tenants) registered on the oBacker service platform. Top-up transactions are processed by bank transfer or payment gateway.

**2.** A Wallet top-up is recorded as a **prepaid service voucher** issued by oBacker to the Client and **does not constitute an advance or a deposit**.

**3.** The Wallet balance is credited once oBacker confirms receipt of the funds in oBacker's bank account. oBacker is not responsible for delays caused by payment processing, the banking system, or errors from a third-party payment gateway beyond oBacker's control.

**4.** The Wallet balance may be used only to pay for oBacker services the Client has registered for. At each billing cycle, the corresponding service fee is **automatically deducted from the Wallet** without any further action from the Client.

**5.** Each top-up has a usage period of **twenty-four (24) months** from the date the top-up is credited to the Wallet. Any balance remaining after this period is automatically recognised as oBacker's service revenue and is **non-refundable**. oBacker notifies the Client **at least 30 days in advance** before any amount expires.

**6.** The Wallet balance is **non-refundable and non-transferable**, unless Vietnamese law provides otherwise or the Client and oBacker agree in writing.

**7.** If the Wallet balance is insufficient to pay a fee that has fallen due, oBacker notifies the Client via the registered contact details at least 03 Business Days in advance under Article 5.3 of the Master T&C. The Services may be suspended until the balance is topped up in full. oBacker is not responsible for any loss or damage arising from suspension of the Services due to an insufficient Wallet balance.

**8.** The minimum top-up amount is **VND 1,000,000** (one million Vietnamese dong) per transaction. oBacker reserves the right to adjust the minimum and maximum top-up limits at its discretion, with notice to the Client before application.

**9.** A VAT e-invoice is issued only when the specific service is performed, matching the actual value of the service deducted from the Wallet. **No VAT e-invoice is issued at the time of top-up.**
